Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026  ·  Last updated: July 2026

Same Same? ("we", "us", "our") is a real-time multiplayer word-association party game available at same-same.fun. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you play Same Same?, how we use it, and your rights.

Short version: We collect only what's needed to run the game. We don't sell your data. You can play as a guest with no account required.

📋 1. Information We Collect

If you create an account (email/password or Google Sign-In):

  • Your email address (used for login only)
  • Your chosen display name (username)
  • Your profile picture / avatar, if you upload one
  • Game statistics: games played, wins, win rate, XP level

If you play as a guest, we only collect:

  • The display name you choose for the session (not stored after you leave)

Automatically, for all players:

  • Gameplay events (e.g. "joined a game", "started a game") sent to PostHog analytics — no personally identifiable information is attached to these events for guest players
  • Standard server logs (IP address, timestamp) generated by our hosting provider (Render)

🔧 2. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the game:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Stores your profile, stats, and avatar. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In (via Supabase Auth) — optional login method. We receive your email and display name from Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud Vision API — used to automatically scan profile pictures for inappropriate content before they are stored. Images are sent to Google for analysis and are not stored by Google after the check.
  • Groq API — powers the in-game AI Judge that validates word submissions during play. Words you submit are sent to Groq for evaluation and are not stored.
  • PostHog — product analytics. Tracks in-game events to help us understand how the game is played. No personally identifiable data is sent for guest sessions. PostHog Privacy Policy
  • Vercel — frontend hosting. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Render — backend hosting. Render Privacy Policy

🎯 3. How We Use Your Information

  • To identify you and save your game progress and statistics
  • To display your username and avatar to other players in shared lobbies
  • To moderate uploaded profile pictures for inappropriate content
  • To validate word submissions using the AI Judge
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it (analytics)
  • To filter usernames and room names for profanity

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with anyone except the services listed above.

🖼️ 4. Profile Pictures (Avatars)

If you upload a profile picture, it is stored in Supabase Storage and displayed to other players in game lobbies. Before storage, it is scanned by the Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch API to detect and reject nudity, violence, or other inappropriate content. We enforce a rate limit of one avatar upload per 7 days per account.

You can delete your avatar at any time by updating your profile in Settings. If you delete your account, your avatar is deleted from storage.

🗄️ 5. Data Retention

  • Account profiles and stats are retained for as long as your account exists
  • Game session data (room state, words submitted during a round) is held in memory only and deleted when the session ends — it is never written to the database
  • Server logs are retained according to Render's standard data retention policy

🌍 6. International Transfers

Same Same? is operated from the EU. Our third-party providers (Supabase, Google, Render, Vercel, Groq, PostHog) may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, these providers operate under appropriate data transfer mechanisms (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

⚖️ 7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data (you can update your username and avatar directly in-app)
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at samesamefun@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

🍪 8. Cookies & Local Storage

Same Same? uses browser Local Storage (not cookies) to remember your session and language preference across visits. No third-party tracking cookies are set. Our analytics provider (PostHog) may use a first-party cookie to identify returning sessions — you can opt out by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser.

👶 9. Children's Privacy

Same Same? is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

🔄 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the date of the latest revision. Continued use of Same Same? after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
